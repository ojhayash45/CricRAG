Law 36 - Out Leg Before Wicket

36.1 Out LBW
A batter is out under this law if the ball, delivered by the bowler and not
touching the bat first, strikes the batter's person without the batter
having made a genuine attempt to hit it with the bat, provided several
conditions about where the ball pitched and where it struck the batter are
also satisfied, and provided the ball would have gone on to hit the
wickets.

36.2 Interception outside off stump
If the batter was outside the line of off stump and was attempting to play
a stroke, this mode of dismissal does not apply, even if every other
condition is met.

36.3 Ball pitching outside leg stump
This mode of dismissal never applies if the ball pitched outside the line
of leg stump, regardless of where it subsequently struck the batter.
